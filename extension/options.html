Meeting AI Notetaker — Settings

Create a token in the web app under Settings → Chrome extension. It is stored only in this browser's extension storage.

Permissions this extension uses
  • tabCapture — to capture the audio of the Google Meet tab, only after you click Start Taking Notes on that tab.
  • offscreen — Chrome requires an offscreen page to hold the audio stream while recording.
  • activeTab — to know the tab you clicked the extension on is a Google Meet call.
  • storage — to remember the server URL, token and recording state.
  • Content script on meet.google.com only — shows the "● Recording" badge on the Meet page and reads the page title to suggest a meeting name.
  • Microphone (optional) — asked separately, only if you want your own voice included.

The extension never records without your action, never captures other tabs, and does not run hidden recording.
